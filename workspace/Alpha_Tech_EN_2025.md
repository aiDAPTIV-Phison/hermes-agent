### V. Management Discussion & Analysis (MD&A)

**5.1 Revenue Decline Explanation**

Consolidated revenue for the quarter was NT$1.284 billion, down 9.1% QoQ, primarily due to:
1. Japanese client Nakamura Precision deferred approximately NT$120 million in Q3 orders to Q4 due to an internal budget review
2. Currency volatility in Southeast Asian markets (depreciation of Thai Baht and Vietnamese Dong) caused certain orders to be re-quoted in USD, delaying transaction timing
3. Intensified competition in Taiwan's domestic semiconductor equipment market resulted in the loss of an NT$85 million order to a competitor

**5.2 Gross Margin Decline Analysis**

Gross margin declined 3.8 percentage points from 34.0% in Q2 to 30.2% this quarter, driven by three key factors:
1. **Rising raw material costs**: Silicon Carbide (SiC) substrate costs rose an average of 12.4% in Q3; supplier price increases could not be fully passed on to customers
2. **Lower capacity utilization**: Due to order deferrals, overall Q3 capacity utilization fell from 87% in Q2 to 74%, increasing fixed cost absorption
3. **Unfavorable product mix**: Shipment share of high-margin precision fixture products declined (Q2: 38% → Q3: 29%), while the share of lower-margin standard components increased

**5.3 Non-Operating Loss Explanation**

Non-operating loss for the quarter was NT$18.2 million, mainly due to:
- Foreign exchange loss: NT$13.4 million (impact of JPY/TWD depreciation)
- Loss on disposal of equipment: NT$4.8 million (retirement of legacy CNC machining equipment)

**5.4 Inventory Increase Explanation**

Inventories increased from NT$689 million in Q2 to NT$762 million (+10.6%). Management has initiated an inventory optimization plan targeting a reduction in inventory days from the current 78 days to below 65 days by Q4.

### VII. Q4 2025 Outlook

- Estimated Q4 Revenue: NT$1.42 ~ 1.50 billion (growth of 10~17% vs. Q3)

### VIII. Risk Factors

1. **Customer Concentration Risk**: Top three customers account for 58% of revenue; single-customer order fluctuations have significant impact
2. **Raw Material Supply Risk**: Only two major suppliers for SiC substrates, limiting bargaining power
3. **Foreign Exchange Risk**: Continued JPY weakness may negatively impact Q4 non-operating income/expense
4. **Intensifying Competition Risk**: Ongoing low-price competition from Chinese manufacturers continues to pressure standard component margins
